ENGINEERED
TO INSPIRE.

The Tesla Roadster 2 is an ambitious electric sports-car concept built around a simple idea: performance can be thrilling while pointing toward a more electric future.

This independent experience explores the design language, publicly discussed goals and possibilities surrounding the Roadster concept. It is not an official Tesla product page.
